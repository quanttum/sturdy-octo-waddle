## Context

A major Canadian bank needed a lending platform and a dealer-facing portal built on Inovatec's loan origination system, integrated with its core banking systems.

## What I did

- Led the pre-sales and implementation team, from solution design to go-live
- Ran delivery with a cross-functional team across product, engineering and the bank's own teams
- Delivered payout quotes, deficiency and document management, full visibility for dealer partners, OEM branding, and support for subvention and other promotions

## Result

Live in nine months, 2.5× faster than comparable projects. The client went on to reach 70% market share, and the account doubled the vendor's revenue from it.
